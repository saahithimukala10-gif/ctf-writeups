# Become a Defender

**Module:** Attacks and Defenses

## Key Points

- Defensive ("blue team") security focuses on detecting, preventing, and responding to attacks, rather than finding and exploiting vulnerabilities directly.
- Core defensive functions: **monitoring** (SIEM, logs, alerts), **incident response** (contain, eradicate, recover once something's detected), and **hardening** (reducing attack surface before anything happens).
- A Security Operations Center (SOC) analyst is a common entry point — triaging alerts, investigating suspicious activity, and escalating real incidents.
- Defense benefits directly from understanding offense: knowing how attackers actually operate (recon, exploitation, privilege escalation) makes it much easier to recognize the signs of each stage inside logs and alerts.

## Remember

Defensive security = detect, respond, and harden rather than exploit. SOC analysts triage alerts and investigate; understanding attacker methodology (from "Become a Hacker") is what makes those alerts make sense.
