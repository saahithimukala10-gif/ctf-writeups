# SOC Metrics and Objectives

**Section:** SOC Team Internals

## Key Points

- SOC metrics measure how well the team is actually doing its job, not just how busy it is — think **Mean Time to Detect (MTTD)**, **Mean Time to Respond/Resolve (MTTR)**, false-positive rate, alert volume, and time to triage.
- Metrics should tie back to real business objectives — protecting specific assets, meeting compliance or SLA requirements — not just exist as numbers for their own sake.
- Bad or "vanity" metrics (like raw count of alerts closed) can incentivize the wrong behavior: rushing closures instead of doing accurate triage.
- Good metrics feed back into process improvement — spotting bottlenecks, justifying more staff or tooling, and showing exactly where a playbook or workbook needs updating.

## Remember

SOC metrics (MTTD, MTTR, false-positive rate, triage time) exist to answer "are we actually getting better," not just "were we busy." Metrics have to map to real objectives, or they end up rewarding the wrong behavior — like rushing closures instead of doing accurate triage.
