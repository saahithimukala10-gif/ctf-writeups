# Humans as Attack Vectors

**Section:** Blue Team Introduction

## Key Points

- People are often the weakest link in a security chain — attackers exploit psychology rather than a technical flaw. This is **social engineering**.
- Common techniques: **phishing** (email), **vishing** (voice/phone calls), **smishing** (SMS), **pretexting** (inventing a scenario to build trust), **baiting** (leaving infected media somewhere tempting), and **tailgating/piggybacking** (physically following someone into a secured area).
- Attackers lean on predictable human tendencies: trust in authority, urgency or fear, curiosity, and the desire to be helpful.
- Defenses here are mostly non-technical: security awareness training, verification procedures (call-backs, out-of-band confirmation), and a culture where reporting a suspicious email isn't embarrassing.

## Remember

Humans are the weakest link because social engineering exploits psychology, not code. Phishing, vishing, smishing, pretexting, baiting, and tailgating all lean on trust, urgency, or curiosity — the fix is awareness training and verification habits, not a patch.
