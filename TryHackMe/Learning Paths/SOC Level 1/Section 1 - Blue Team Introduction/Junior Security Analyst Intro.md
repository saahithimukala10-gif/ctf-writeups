# Junior Security Analyst Intro

**Section:** Blue Team Introduction

## Key Points

- Frames the junior security analyst role as the org's "first line of defense" — monitoring and investigating security alerts as threats come in.
- Walks through a typical SOC team: a senior analyst, a SOC engineer, a manager, and an incident responder, each with a different slice of the workflow.
- Shows that the role is collaborative by design — a junior analyst investigates and escalates, rather than working every incident alone end-to-end.
- Includes a "Day in the Life" lab: reviewing real-looking alerts and answering investigation questions, to get a feel for the actual day-to-day work.

## Remember

Junior analyst = first line of defense: monitor alerts, investigate, and escalate to the right teammate rather than solving everything solo. The SOC is a team sport — senior analyst, engineer, manager, and incident responder all play different roles.
