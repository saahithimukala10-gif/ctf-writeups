# SOC Role in Blue Team

**Section:** Blue Team Introduction

## Key Points

- Maps the security reporting hierarchy: technical staff → managers → CISO → CEO, showing where a SOC analyst sits in the bigger picture.
- Defines the **Blue Team** as the defensive side of security — SOC analysts, engineers, and incident responders — as opposed to the offensive Red Team.
- SOC is the org's first line of defense; escalated or serious incidents get handed off to a specialized team like CIRT (Cyber Incident Response Team).
- Contrasts an **internal SOC** (in-house team) with an **MSSP** (Managed Security Service Provider — an outsourced SOC), which changes what day-to-day work and career growth look like.
- Covers realistic career progression advice for entry-level analysts: build practical experience, learn from every alert, and understand how internal vs. outsourced SOCs differ.

## Remember

Blue Team = defense (SOC analysts, engineers, incident responders) vs. Red Team = offense. SOC is the first line of defense; CIRT handles what gets escalated. Internal SOC (in-house) vs. MSSP (outsourced) is a real distinction that shapes the job.
